I came across a problem, which I could not enhance my venv dependencies to my project so that VScode did not show the hilight on libraries's name.

I was looking for the solution and I found it. The good reference video is the following link.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JYdd1k44FRM

Easily
1. Open command palet
2. Put 'environment' and find the key word of 'Create Virtual Environment'
3. Select your venv folder. In that case traditionally putting venv name as `.venv` is better in terms of setting and you can follow the video as well
